About Afra

A year in, and still obsessed with getting the details right

I started out doing makeup for family functions and close friends in Melapalayam, and one year later it's grown into full bridal bookings, engagement looks and party makeup across Tamil Nadu. I'm still early in my journey, which is exactly why I spend so much time on the basics that most people rush past — skin prep, colour matching, and making sure the look holds up in photos, not just in the mirror.

Every face is different, so I don't work off one fixed "signature look." I ask what you're comfortable with, look at your outfit and the lighting of your event, and build the makeup around that — never the other way around.

  • Skin prep & long-wear base, matched to your actual skin tone
  • Bridal, engagement, reception & party makeup
  • HD and airbrush-friendly finishes for photography
  • Hygienic tools and skin-friendly, tested products
